RESEARCH OVERVIEW

Intelligent IT Ticket Decision Support and Triage System

Evidence-first research and engineering work for traceable IT ticket decision support.

01 / PROBLEM

IT Support Ticket Triage

IT support and software-maintenance teams receive large numbers of partially structured, noisy and historically evolving tickets. Triage requires understanding tickets, estimating priority and severity, supporting routing, identifying duplicates, retrieving related historical tickets and estimating time to resolution.

These signals do not all have the same semantics, timestamp, reliability, missingness or provenance. The research therefore treats ticket intelligence as a collection of task-specific evidence problems rather than as one universal prediction problem.

02 / OBJECTIVE

Evidence-First Decision Support

The project develops an evidence-first, leakage-controlled and confidence-aware IT ticket decision-support system using task-specific predictive and retrieval models.

Native output meaning is preserved for each task. The system does not assume a universal probability, confidence or risk quantity across all tasks.

03 / RESEARCH WORKFLOW

From Dataset Investigation to Controlled Modeling

  1. Source discovery and provenance
  2. Dataset audit and integrity investigation
  3. Field and temporal characterization
  4. Formal Dataset Contract and Dataset Task Map
  5. Task-specific modeling contracts
  6. Controlled baselines and modeling
  7. Evidence integration and decision-support boundaries

07 / EVIDENCE INTEGRATION

Preserve Native Evidence Meaning

Evidence integration is designed as a presentation and decision-support layer while preserving each task's native semantics, grain, confidence or uncertainty, missingness and provenance.

Missing Evidence

Unavailable evidence is not treated as negative evidence.

Native Grain

Task-specific output meaning is preserved.

No Universal Confidence

Cross-task confidence normalization is not established.

No Universal Risk

A universal cross-task risk score is not established.

08 / LIMITATIONS & BOUNDARIES

What the Research Does Not Claim

  • Historical assignment is not proof of current routing correctness.
  • T6 observable non-positive retrieval evidence is not a true negative.
  • T7 censoring must not be treated as ordinary unresolved negatives.
  • T5 retains a documented provenance limitation.
  • No universal confidence or risk score has been established.
  • No empirically validated autonomous automation threshold has been established.
  • T11 does not claim an operational feedback population where none currently exists.
09 / RESEARCH CONCLUSION

Evidence Before Automation

The resulting system is structured around task-specific evidence rather than a single universal prediction or risk score.

The research establishes a traceable chain from dataset investigation through task-specific modeling and evidence integration, while retaining explicit limitations and a human decision boundary.
